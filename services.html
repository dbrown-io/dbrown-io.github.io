BEFORE YOU BOOK
  • You get a price before I start. I tell you the number, you say yes, then I do the work. You pay once it’s finished, not before.
  • Dog jobs start with a walk you come on. I don’t take anyone’s dog out on my own until we’ve done one together and you’ve seen how I handle them.
  • A parent knows where I am. Every job, the address and the time I’m due back. It keeps me safe and it means someone else always knows the arrangement.
  • You can ask for a reference. If you’d rather hear it from someone who has already booked me, say so and I’ll put you in touch.
